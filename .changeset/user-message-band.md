`/hl` no longer paints your messages as a bright yellow (or orange-red) block. A message now sits on a faint band — the terminal's own background stepped a little lighter or darker — only as tall as its text, with the `■` mark in the user colour (it was invisible on the old block) and the text at normal weight. The band is on by default; a choice made earlier with `/hl` is kept. A very long message (a pasted log) shows its first 12 and last 4 lines with the rest counted. Switching between dark and light now recolours messages already on screen.
