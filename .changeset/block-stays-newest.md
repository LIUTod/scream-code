While a turn runs, its folded block stays the newest thing on screen. Notices the work raises — a background command finishing, a skill the model activated, the reply hitting the output limit, a status or warning line, the approval of a subagent's call — become rows of the block instead of landing below it and pushing it up. An image read is a row too: one line while folded, its thumbnail inside the tree once expanded (on terminals that draw images). Things that belong to the conversation — a message you send mid-turn, a question and its answer, a cron prompt, a hook result, compaction — close the block first, and the work after them opens a new block underneath.
